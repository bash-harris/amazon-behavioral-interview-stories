## 3. Biggest gaps (ranked)

1. **LP-fit shortfall on the primary.** Have Backbone's strength indicators (rubric §8) are written around peers and supervisors and around *tenacious* challenge. The story shows (a) yielding to a domain expert and (b) responding to customer distrust with a feature. Neither half required holding an unpopular position under pressure from a colleague. The "strong signals" section even softens the rubric text from "peers and supervisors" to "others" — a Bar Raiser reading both side-by-side will catch the paraphrase.
2. **Results are anecdote-only at Q6's chosen sourcing.** Everything quantifiable in the answer is "two edge cases, first week." No count of engineers involved, no baseline of how often they had hand-verified, no before/after of verification time — because the usable numbers (20–30 min/board, 15 engineers, 20 h/week) live in STORY 1/other stories deliberately held elsewhere. The story's own Result section (L1057–1059) contains no scale metric, so "Results — concrete, measurable" scores weak *by construction*. This is a sourcing constraint, not a fabrication problem — but it must be acknowledged and bounded, not papered over.
3. **"Re-verified by hand" is an attribution slip.** The cited STORY 11 text says the engineers were *hesitant to rely on the tool for critical decisions* (L1041). "They re-verified by hand — which defeats an automation tool" is sourced from adjacent stories (PREP.md Story 10 L387: an engineer spent 30 min manually verifying one false positive; PREPARATION.md STORY 10 L973 implies routine manual pin-count verification) — both outside Q6's declared fact set ("Only STORY 11 and STORY 1 variant-semantics facts are used"). Either cite the supporting story or soften the claim.
4. **"Became willing to rely… for production" upgrades the source.** L1059 says "**more** willing to rely." The answer drops the comparative and asserts a categorical position change ("That changed their position… they were willing to rely"). On aggressive questioning ("all of them? stopped hand-checking entirely? how many?") the categorical version collapses; the comparative version does not.
5. **No alternatives, costs, or dissent on the debug view itself.** The source bank actually contains mined-ready judgment material Q6 left unused: query-param-vs-UI-toggle rationale (L1071) and localhost-only exposure as a security decision (L1083). As delivered, the influence act looks costless and uncontested — missing-trade-offs is an explicit adversarial flag in the run spec.
