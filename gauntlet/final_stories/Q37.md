## How to pick 3-4 anchors (the actual note-taking rule)

- **Take 1 as the headline** (Anchor 1): proudest = the thing that stopped being my project and became the team's workflow. Q9's core already says this - reuse its wording, don't re-cook it.
- **Fill to 3-4 by coverage, not volume**: one delivery/architecture win (Q8 - how the tool reached the users), one measurement-rigor win (Q27 - how carefully the numbers were proved), optional one judgment/simplicity win (Q54 - a good call about what NOT to build). Three deep anchors beat six shallow ones.
- **Each anchor gets six jotted lines only** (already in its source file's Memory Anchors): the constraint, the option rejected, the number + what kind of number it is (measured, estimated, or just reported), the trade-off paid (what I gave up to get it), the result, and one "do differently." If a detail isn't in the source file, it doesn't go on the note.
- **Spoken-core number cap still applies**: 1-3 numbers per story when told. This note is the shelf they come from, not the answer.
